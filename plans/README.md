### Direction (options for Kevin, not ranked against the bugs)

- **The battle at 26 cards (plan 026, spike).** Eight cards cannot battle: their pages have no Battle button and the
  dock cannot pick them, while their battle sound cues (1.36 MB), Arena figures (0.93 MB), move footage (9.3 MB) and
  worlds (10.7 MB) already ship unused. The dry run validated all 26, but the kit rebuild would take battle-r4b's
  unfinished, uncommitted work with it. The spike measures three routes and designs the move-id map for the 12 moves
  whose card name and battle name differ. It is the one item that advances goals 1, 2 and 3 together.
- **Real logos (plan 027).** Two cards print something that is not their product's logo (Kevin Wiki, 050525), one
  logo's origin is unrecorded (Podium), and 11 marks read under 3:1 on their tile. Every open "Kevin decides" line
  goes on one sheet. Printing Kevin's portrait mark needs his explicit exception to the no-photos rule.
- **A card page plays its card's sound (plan 028).** All 26 cards have a `sig.<id>.lift` cue that only the wall
  plays; a visitor who lands on a card from a shared link hears nothing. Trade-off: 84 KB of player on the site's
  lightest pages, fetched only at the press.
- **Move previews on card pages (plan 029).** 144 preview files ship for the battle's tooltips; 60 of the 72 moves on
  fielded cards resolve by name. The card page would show each move happening, next to its Battle button. It makes
  the 18-of-26 gap more visible until 026 lands.
- **Claude of Tanks' October footage in the battle (plan 030, spike).** The handoff's four move clips (plus one trait
  clip) wait for the merge; the spike finds which can ship before the kit rebuild. Goal 4's strongest showing on the
  site.
