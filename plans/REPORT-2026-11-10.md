1. **Translation run for 029.** One string: `card.playPreview` = `Play the battle preview of {move}`. It is the
   accessible name of a button in a card page's Moves list; `{move}` stays as written; "Play" means play a video.
   After `pnpm i18n:translate`, `b61b848` can be merged.
2. **26 cards in the battle (026).** Recommended: rebuild `kit.js` from the committed tree with the 26-card engine
   and let the eight staged cards play default scenes until their own scenes pass the flash scan. Costs: the 18
   fielded cards need a re-check (19 to 33 hours of machine time at the loaded rate), the Arena's figure manifest
   must carry the eight in the same step, and saved replay links change. The seven questions are at the end of
   `plans/results/026-battle-26-cards.md`.
3. **Claude of Tanks footage (030).** Ship Sabot Round, One Spec and 14 v 14 now and Killcam with the kit rebuild,
   or all four with the kit. Three questions close `plans/results/030-claude-of-tanks-footage.md`.
4. **Logos (027).** Seven questions in `plans/results/027-logo-decisions.md`; the private sheet with the pictures is
   `~/k7-rescue/improve-audit/logo-decisions/index.html`.
5. **Music on phones (022).** Themes are decoded whole on every iPhone (28.5 MB while a hand plays). Streaming them
   on touch screens would save that and lose loop points, lap changes and the Final Turns layer there.
6. **`llms.txt` (024).** It no longer has an About heading: the facts sit above the first `##`, as the convention
   asks. Restoring the heading is a one-line revert plus a change to the guard's new check.
7. **The battle's interface flicker between shots (023).** The most visible pop left. The fix belongs in the round
   (`data-scene` set for the batch, not for each shot) and needs a design look at each rule that reads it.
